# Survive The Night

For any work on models, the first-person viewmodel (hands and held items), anything a survivor holds or wears,
ground pickups, or where the world places its props: read and follow [docs/object-clipping.md](docs/object-clipping.md).
It is the workflow (survey, fix, re-measure, before/after, check in the game) and the tools in `scripts/clip/`.

The codebase is described in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

To open a pull request, follow [.claude/skills/create-pr/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/create-pr/SKILL.md): a short overview for a
non-technical reader, screenshots of the change, a Risk section for server changes and a metrics table for tuning.
