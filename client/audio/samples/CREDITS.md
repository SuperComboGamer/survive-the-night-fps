# Audio recordings: credits and licence

Every recording in this folder is **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**, from [Freesound](https://freesound.org).
No attribution is legally required; the authors are credited here anyway. The licence of each source was
verified on its Freesound page when the Deadfall asset set was built (Freesound HQ previews, CC0 only).

The files were prepared for Deadfall (filtered, sliced into multi-take sprites, loops made seamless) and then
re-encoded for this game: ambience beds trimmed to 40-60 s with an equal-power seamless loop point and encoded as
32 kHz Ogg Vorbis, one-shots re-encoded as mono Ogg Vorbis. Slice tables live in `../samples.js`.

The gunshots (`gun_*`), bullet impacts (`hit_bullet`, `hit_head`) and zombie voices (`zv_*`) were sourced for this
game from Freesound HQ previews, each licence checked as CC0 on the sound's own page (September 2026). Each take is
trimmed to start on its transient, high-passed, level-matched and soft-limited; the full-auto takes are single shots
from a real burst spliced onto that burst's natural tail; `zv_idle` is looped with an equal-power crossfade.

The score (`mus_*`), the cinematic stingers (`stg_*`) and the rest of the sound set - the idle moans and the voices
of the special infected (`zv_moan`, `zv_boss`, `zv_screech`, `zv_spit`, `zv_gurgle`, `zv_whisper`, and the rebuilt
`zv_roar`), the horde bed (`amb_horde`), dogs and the cat, the survivors' own voice (`pv_*`, one performer), weapon
handling and small foley (`fol_*`), explosions and impacts (`exp_*`, `imp_*`), rain and thunder, the horn and the car -
were sourced the same way (Freesound HQ previews, each licence read off the sound's own page as CC0, October 2026;
derivative uploads whose sources were not themselves CC0, AI-generated uploads and recordings of other people's
performances were left out). Music stems are stereo 32 kHz loops: cut on their bar lines where the piece has a tempo
(the taiko at 110 bpm, the boss theme on its 40-bar repeat at 100 bpm), crossfaded where it has none, and mastered to
the level they play at. Voices and foley are trimmed to their onsets, high-passed, level-matched per take and
soft-limited; a few are resampled (the tank's snarl is pitched down in the file) or assembled from short takes (the
spitter's hock-and-spit, the boomer's gurgles).
